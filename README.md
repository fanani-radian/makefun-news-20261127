## The image

`entry.png` — 1024×1024 PNG, 197,661 bytes, sha256
`05febb06da14df5154965469c38f6189f4c798df6a36b8f495f9abcc828f1df9`.

A dense front page: lead illustration of the Jazan refinery fire, 20 stories
across 4 regions, a numbers panel, a WITA timeline strip, a pin map of
10 cities, 4 "Also today" briefs, and a date-check line. Every story
carries a sourcing tag; every story was verified against two named sources
and happened, or was first reported, on Friday 27 November 2026 in some
time zone.

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies the bundled `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and verifies size and sha256
after the copy. If `entry.png` is missing it attempts a redraw via
`draw.py`, fully guarded so a missing PIL/fonts can never break the copy
path. `draw.py` renders the image fresh with Pillow.

## Date rule applied

The competition counts events that happened, or were first reported, on
27 November 2026 **in any time zone**. Items first reported 11:00–13:00 UTC
on 26 Nov are included because 27 Nov had already begun in New Zealand and
Tonga (UTC+13/+14): the Odesa grain ships, the IRGC mine claim, the Kim
aid-suspension claim, and the Meloni/EU summit story. Everything else drawn
was first reported from ~13:00 UTC 26 Nov onward, or on 27 Nov UTC.
